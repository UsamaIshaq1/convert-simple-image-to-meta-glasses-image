# Meta Photo Converter

Convert your simple images into Meta glasses-ready photos — right from your browser.

🌐 **Live demo:** https://convert-simple-image-to-meta-glasse.vercel.app/

## What it does

Meta Photo Converter takes a regular image and converts it into a format optimized for Meta glasses, so you can view and share your photos on your device. No sign-up, no installs — just open the site and convert.

## Features

- Upload any image from your device
- One-click conversion to Meta glasses format
- Download the converted photo instantly
- 100% browser-based — your images never leave your device
- Free to use, no account required

## How to use

1. Open https://convert-simple-image-to-meta-glasse.vercel.app/
2. Upload the image you want to convert
3. Hit convert, then download your Meta-ready photo

## Run it locally

No build step needed — it's plain HTML/CSS/JS.

```bash
# Option 1: just open the file
open meta-photo.html

# Option 2: serve it locally
npx serve .
```

Then visit `http://localhost:3000/meta-photo.html` in your browser.

## Deploy your own copy

This project is deployed on [Vercel](https://vercel.com). To deploy your own:

1. Fork or clone this repo
2. Import it into Vercel (or run `vercel` from the project folder)
3. Done — Vercel auto-deploys on every push to `main`

> Tip: rename `meta-photo.html` to `index.html` if you want it to load automatically at the root URL.
